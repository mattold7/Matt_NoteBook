# Trek Roscoe – Rear Wheel Replacement

## Problem
- Rear wheel is **bent**.
- Son keeps getting **flats**. We think the rim flexing is causing them.

## Bike specs
- Wheel size: **27.5"**
- Front: Boost 110. On 27.5" Roscoes this is usually a **15mm thru-axle** (Maxle), not a true QR. Front wheel is fine, so this doesn't matter for now.
- Rear: **Boost 141, 5mm QR**
- Drivetrain: **12-speed**. Brand still to confirm (check the rear derailleur).

## Wheel to buy: Bontrager Kovee TLR Boost 27.5" rear (141mm QR)
Direct replacement, no adapters needed. Order through a Trek dealer.

| Cassette on bike | Freehub version to order | Approx. price |
|---|---|---|
| SRAM SX / NX Eagle 12-speed | Shimano HG 8/9/10-speed | ~$220 |
| Shimano Deore / SLX 12-speed | Shimano Micro Spline 12-speed | ~$258 |

- SRAM's budget 12-speed cassettes fit an HG freehub. Many 2020–2022 Roscoe 7/8s shipped this way.
- Rim: 22.5mm internal, tubeless-ready.
- **Not included:** skewer, rim strip, valve. Reuse the old skewer.
- Plus tires (2.8") were meant for a wider rim (Line 40). The Kovee is better with 2.4–2.6" tires.

### Step-up option
Boost 148 wheelset (e.g. DT Swiss 350 hubs) plus the **QR end-cap kit that matches the freehub**, to convert it to 141 QR. Micro Spline QR caps are a different part from HG/XD. The caps run about $55 extra.

## Flats – what to check
A bent rim usually isn't what causes flats. Look at where the hole is on the tube:
- **Inner side (facing the rim)** → rim tape shifted or a spoke hole is exposed.
- **Two side-by-side slits ("snakebite")** → pinch flat from low pressure.
- **Outer side** → thorn or debris. Run your fingers around the inside of the tire to find it.
- A **dent in the rim edge** can cut tubes. The new wheel fixes that.
- Check tire pressure before rides.

## Best fix for flats: go tubeless
- Kovee rim is tubeless-ready. Needs tubeless rim tape, a tubeless valve, and sealant (~$30–40).
- Sealant plugs thorn punctures, and with no tube there's nothing to pinch.
- Confirm the tires are tubeless-ready (most Bontrager trail tires are).
