### Hitch notes

- Use the Metal Bumper variant. The plastic-bumper listing will not fit the Badlands' steel bumper.
- Coating: bed liner instead of powder coat (thicker and more chip resistant). A spray-in liner builds up thickness, so mask the receiver bore, the bolt holes and the mating surfaces, since HK's bolt tolerances are tight. Ask the shop about prepping raw steel (sandblast or scuff and primer), minimum charge and turnaround. A bed liner shop near Pflugerville, TX: Texas Truck Coatings, 9099 Research Blvd, Austin, 737-383-8807 (spray-in liner reviews). The powder coat shops I found earlier remain a fallback: Crosslink Powder Coating of Austin (Pflugerville, 512-989-6458), Diamond Powder Coating (512-373-8782), Brooks Industrial Coatings (512-990-5333).
- Torque specs: 60 ft-lbs for the frame points and 82 ft-lbs for the center hardware. Start all bolts loose, then tighten. Bolt-hole tolerances are tight.
- The hitch rating does not raise the Bronco's tow rating.
- The HK hitch has no lighting. Trailer turn signals and brake lights run through the trailer brake module. It may need activation with FORScan or Ford's dealer software, and a separate brake controller is only needed for electric-brake trailers.

### Hardtop notes

- All-aluminum, 3M-bonded front fairing, 70% tint glass. XM signal is blocked by the roof, so reroute the antenna or stream by Bluetooth.
- Confirm 2027 fitment and clearance with the 360-degree camera and rear sensors.
- The hardtop weight isn't listed, so plan for helpers or a hoist.
- Suggested order of work: film first (if any), then the hitch, then the hardtop.
