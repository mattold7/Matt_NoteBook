### Things to question or confirm with the dealer

1. **$165 in-transit fee.** Ford's $1,995 destination charge already covers delivery to the dealer. Ask what this is for and whether it can be dropped.
2. **Itemized breakdown of $53,706.91.** Ask what discounts or rebates make up the difference from MSRP, and whether destination is already included.
3. **Stabilizer bar disconnect ($1,305).** Ford's 2027 order guide confirms 68M is a standalone option on Badlands and is included in the Sasquatch Package (765). Fine without Sasquatch, redundant if Sasquatch is added.
4. **Manual transmission tradeoffs.** Per Ford's order guide, the 7-speed adds Granny Gear, Crank in Gear and Hill Descent Control, while the 10-speed adds Trail Control, Trail Turn Assist and Trail One-Pedal Driving. The manual also means the 2.3L only, since the 3.0L V6 (99N) is automatic-only, and per the Lux listing in the guide, Remote Start System and Ambient Footwell and Rear Lighting are automatic-only (confirm whether Ford App remote start works on the manual).
5. **Block heater ($190).** Only worth it with regular sustained freezing temperatures.
6. **Intentionally left off:** Sasquatch Package (35" tires), the 3.0L EcoBoost engine (new for 2027 on Badlands), HOSS 3.0 suspension, and a hardtop from Ford.
7. **Tow package.** The quote lists none. Ford's order guide shows the Trailer Tow Prep Pack as standard, and the hitch receiver itself is the dealer-installed Trailer Tow Package (no ball or ball mount included). Skipping it fits the HK hitch plan, but ask what wiring the prep pack provides.
8. **Seats and flooring (decided).** Keeping the standard 6-way manual marine grade vinyl seats with the standard rubberized, washout-capable flooring. The power seats come only with the Dark Khaki vinyl option (PV, about $595 per a dealer site), which replaces the rubberized flooring with carpet, so PV is skipped.

### Cross-check against Ford's 2027 Bronco Order Guide (dated 10/13/26)

**Confirmed**

- Every code on the quote matches the Badlands 4-Door section: E9B, 99H, 44Q, 334A, VA, 68M, 68S (which includes LED taillamps 67Q), 63C, 41H and 153.
- 4.7 final drive with electronic-locking front and rear axles is standard with the manual. The 10-speed gets 4.46, and 4.7 on the automatic requires Sasquatch.
- Badlands standard equipment includes powder-coated steel front and rear bumpers, full steel bash plates, rock rails, front (2) and rear (2) tow hooks, a full-size spare, LT285/70R17 R/T 33" tires, HOSS 2.0 and G.O.A.T. modes including Rock-Crawl and Baja.
- "CALIF EMISSIONS NOT REQUIRED" is the control code (423) for a truck shipped to a California-emissions-state dealer and registered elsewhere. Fine if you are registering in a state that is not on the guide's California-emissions list.
- The front license plate bracket is standard in two-plate states and optional elsewhere, so $0 here is expected.

**Owner priorities (all met by the current order):** 7-speed manual, electronic-locking front and rear axles (standard with the manual, 4.7 gears), front stabilizer bar disconnect (68M), and the highest equipment group (Lux, 334A).

**Optional tech add-ons beyond Lux (decided: none).** Skipping the B&O 12-speaker upgrade (58P), the 7-year Ford Connectivity Package (91D) and the SiriusXM 3-year plan (91T). The order stays as quoted, with Lux's included 10-speaker B&O system and the 1-year Connectivity Package.

**2027 Badlands changes worth knowing**

- LED signature headlamps and LED taillamps are no longer standard, which is why 68S is a $595 option.
- The center-console passenger grab handle was removed from standard equipment.
- Fuel economy, horsepower and torque are still listed as TBD in the guide.

## 2. Planned Aftermarket (installed by owner after delivery)

### [Armadillo Designs Four Door Bronco Squareback Hardtop 2.0](https://armadillo.design/products/four-door-bronco-squareback-hardtop)

- **Price:** $6,500. **Listed lead time:** 12 weeks. **Availability:** both Standard and Raptor variants showed _sold out_ when checked, so call 281-654-8958 to confirm.
- All-aluminum with a satin black powder coat, 70% tint DOT glass, removable roof panels, gullwing side doors, a rear door that sits about 10% higher than OEM, and about 2" more headroom.
- Permanent install. The front fairing is attached with 3M adhesive, and no soft top or OEM hardtop brackets are used. It is still fully removable.
- Rear defroster is included but the **wiring harness is sold separately**.
- **XM radio signal is blocked** by the hardtop. The fix is to reroute the antenna through the access port, or stream through Bluetooth. The quote includes a SiriusXM trial.
- Confirm: 2027 model-year fitment (not stated on the page), clearance with the 360-degree camera and any rear sensors, installation instructions, and whether a DIY install affects the warranty.
- The cloth soft top on the order was $0, so nothing is recovered by skipping it. It could be sold.

### [HK Offroad Rear Tow Hitch (Class III, 5,000 lb rating)](https://www.hkoffroad.com/products/ford-bronco-2021-rear-tow-hitch-w-shackle-tabs)

Product page: https://www.hkoffroad.com/products/ford-bronco-2021-rear-tow-hitch-w-shackle-tabs The link originally provided (Plastic Bumper, Standard Hitch variant): https://www.hkoffroad.com/products/ford-bronco-2021-rear-tow-hitch-w-shackle-tabs?variant=41999054471214

- **Link issue:** the link provided was the _Plastic Bumper, Standard Hitch_ ($438). Ford's order guide confirms the Badlands comes with a powder-coated steel rear bumper, and HK says the plastic version will not fit a factory steel bumper, so choose a Metal Bumper option:
    - Metal Bumper Standard (no tabs): **$458**
    - Metal Bumper Deluxe (with shackle tabs): **$558** (the Badlands already has two rear tow hooks, so the tabs are optional and mainly add D-ring points on the hitch itself)
- Ships as raw steel, so paint or powder coat is needed.
- Requires a **locking pin** (a cotter pin will not fit).
- Reviewers report tight bolt-hole tolerances. HK's torque specs are 60 ft-lbs for the frame points and 82 ft-lbs for the center hardware. Start all bolts loose, then tighten.
- One reviewer reported incompatibility with a MagnaFlow exhaust. A reviewer with a 2024 Badlands said Ford changed the bumper brackets that year, so ask HK about 2027 fitment.
- The hitch rating does not raise the vehicle's tow rating. The Bronco's own rating is the legal limit.
- The quote doesn't include Ford's dealer-installed Trailer Tow Package (the factory receiver), which is what this hitch replaces. The Trailer Tow Prep Pack is standard, but the guide doesn't say what wiring it includes, so ask HK and the dealer how trailer wiring will work.
- Delivery is currently averaging about two months.

## 4. DIY Install Notes

- **Hitch:** easy install per reviewers. Use a second person. Torque to the specs above.
- **Hardtop:** the harder job. The page doesn't list its weight, so plan for helpers or a hoist and one clean placement onto the adhesive. Run the defroster wiring yourself.
