# Bella Vista Build and Retirement Plan

Working notes from a planning session on 2026-11-01. Figures marked **(est.)** are my rough estimates, not quotes. Not tax, legal, or financial advice; confirm with your lender and CPA.

## Situation

- Age 43. Kids graduate in about 6 years. Target retirement at 55 (about 12 years out), so a retirement horizon near 40 years.
- Pflugerville home: bought for $615k, about 5 years lived in, owe about $415k. 5/1 ARM, first reset Feb 2027, roughly 2% cap per year (from you, not verified against the note). Current payment $3,031.68.
- Bella Vista lots: 1 acre, 0.5 acre, 0.27 acre. Planned build on the **0.27 acre** lot because it is close to Gear Gardens, Razorback Greenway, Oz Bike Park, and Blowing Springs.
- Planned build: 2,857 sq ft at $250/sq ft = about **$714,250** (builder quote).
- Funding on hand: about $80k lot equity, $300k earmarked, $150k emergency fund (kept separate), about $162k Schwab taxable.
- You are a woodworker and will build the furniture yourself. All equipment is in Pflugerville.

## Short-term rental (STR) findings

- **Bella Vista permit cap blocks a new STR.** The city caps non-owner-occupied permits at 600. The city's page says the cap was reached in July 2026 and new applications go on a waiting list. A proposal to raise it to 687 failed 1-4 on July 27, 2026. Permits are non-transferable.
- Owner-occupied rentals are exempt, but "owner-occupied" means it is your primary residence and you are present during the stay.
- **Tax loophole basics.** Average guest stay of 7 days or less plus material participation can make losses non-passive and offset W-2 income. 100% bonus depreciation is permanent for qualifying property acquired after Jan 19, 2025, and cost segregation speeds deductions.
- **Why it does not fit you.** Remote ownership (about a 9-hour drive) makes 500 hours, or 100+ hours and more than anyone else, very hard. Heavy family use trips the personal-use limit (more than 14 days or 10% of rental days). Free stays by friends and family count as personal use and are not rental days.
- Market data conflicts: Rabbu shows about 24% occupancy, $163 average daily rate, roughly $24.5k a year per listing. RedAwning shows 56% and $185. Treat the high figures with caution.
- **Conclusion:** keep the first house for personal use, with no STR assumption.

## Long-term rent in Bella Vista

- 3 bedroom about $2,078, 4+ bedroom about $2,760 to $2,800, average house about $2,195. About 12% of residents rent.
- Building rental houses on the other lots yields roughly 4% (est.), below the cost of an investment loan. Not recommended as a pure income play.

## Corrections to the original master plan

- STR is not "passive loss trapped" in every case; the real blockers are the permit cap and personal use.
- Section 121 exclusion: already met (lived in the home 2 of the last 5 years). It excludes gain, not proceeds, and renting it for more than about 3 years before selling loses it.
- The 14.83% and 15.91% DTI figures leave out the new mortgage.
- "2% cap guarantees ~$3,450" only holds if 2% is also the first-adjustment cap.
- The $300k recast is not guaranteed; get it in writing.
- The $8.8M projection is in future dollars (about $6.2M today at 3% inflation). A 3.5% withdrawal is about $216k a year in today's dollars, not $308k.
- Missing: health insurance before Medicare, early-retirement pension reduction, penalty-free access before 59½.
- A pension reduces sequence risk only in proportion to how much spending it covers.
- Umbrella insurance adds liability coverage but does not "isolate" the portfolio.
- Do not retitle 401(k)s or IRAs into a trust; use beneficiary designations.
- Stray "[health]" text in the retirement section.

## High-earner tax moves beyond 401(k) and backdoor Roth

2026 limits: 401(k) deferral $24,500; total defined-contribution limit $72,000; HSA family $8,750 (+$1,000 at 55); IRA $7,500.

1. **Mega backdoor Roth** if his plan allows after-tax contributions with in-plan conversion.
2. **Taxable bridge account** for ages 55 to 59½. Check Rule of 55 on his 401(k).
3. **Roth conversion plan** for the low-income years after retirement; about 20 years before required distributions.
4. **HSA**, invested, with receipts saved.
5. **Charity**: donor-advised fund funded with appreciated stock; 2026 adds a 0.5% of AGI floor and a 35% cap on itemized benefit for 37% taxpayers.
6. **Itemizing check**: SALT cap is $40,400 for 2026 (phases out above about $505k); mortgage interest limited to $750k of combined debt across both homes.
7. Ask HR about a non-qualified deferred comp plan and ESPP.

## Other questions covered

- **LLC for furniture:** not worth it. Furniture for your own house is personal; an LLC does not make it deductible. A real woodworking business with customers is a separate decision.
- **Travel and vehicles:** deductible only if the property is rented and trips are primarily for the rental. Do not buy a vehicle for the deduction.
- **Shop:** none in this first build. Plan is a house plus detached shop on the 1-acre lot later. Verify city and Architectural Control Committee rules on shop size, height, materials, and business use before relying on it.

## Long-term idea: two rentals plus a forever home

Plan: at retirement, rent both Pflugerville and the Bella Vista 0.27 house, then build the forever home on the 1-acre lot.

| Monthly, today's rents (est.) | Pflugerville | Bella Vista |
|---|---|---|
| Rent assumption | $2,900 | $2,780 |
| After management, vacancy, upkeep (~19%) | $2,350 | $2,250 |
| Loan P&I plus taxes, insurance, POA | $3,920 | $3,700 |
| Gap | about -$1,570 | about -$1,450 |

- Combined shortfall about $36k a year, of which roughly $13k is principal paid down.
- The 2.3% rate advantage of keeping Pflugerville is mostly gone after the ARM reset. Pflugerville's median sale price was about $380,750 in April 2026, flat year over year.
- Forever-home financing is hard with no W-2 income; building while employed is easier.
- In retirement, up to $25k of passive losses can offset other income when MAGI is below about $100k (phasing out by $150k). This conflicts with large Roth conversions.
- Renting a second-home-financed house can breach loan terms; living in it for a year or more before renting generally avoids that, but confirm with Arvest.
- **Middle path:** keep one rental, sell Pflugerville at about year 6, and use the cash to pay down Bella Vista or help fund the forever home.
